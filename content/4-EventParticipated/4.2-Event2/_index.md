# Summary Report: "AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application"

### 1. Event Objectives

The **AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application** event was organized to connect the AWS Study Group community with industry experts. The event focused on sharing Cloud market trends in Vietnam, AWS's vision, and career orientation for students participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program.

### 2. Speakers

The event featured experienced professionals from AWS and AWS partner companies:

- **Hung**: AWS representative, shared AWS's vision and Cloud development direction.
- **Vinh** from Renova Cloud: Shared practical experience in the Cloud industry.
- **Nhu** and **Khang**: Swinburne alumni, shared perspectives from university learning to enterprise work.

### 3. Key Highlights

#### Cloud market in Vietnam

The demand for Cloud talent in Vietnam is growing strongly, but skill requirements are also becoming increasingly higher . The appearance of large Talent Hubs, such as NAB's Talent Hub, shows the strong development potential of Cloud in Vietnam.

#### Recruitment culture

One notable point was that 90-100% of job opportunities at large corporations such as AWS come from internal recruitment and referrals, instead of only public job postings . This shows that networking and proactively building a personal profile are very important.

#### Differences between school and enterprise environments

School projects usually have clean data, clear requirements, and stable scope. In real enterprises, data is often messy, requirements change continuously, and engineers need strong adaptability .

### 4. Lessons Learned

- **Problem-solving mindset:** Instead of memorizing concepts, learners should ask "Why?" to understand the technical nature of a problem .
- **Soft skills:** IT work is not only about coding. Engineers also need to collaborate with business departments to understand business value .
- **Using AI:** AI is a helpful tool, but relying on it without understanding the fundamentals can become a weakness when facing real enterprise assessments .

### 5. Work Applications

- **Building a profile:** Do not stop at completing assignments. Actively optimize and expand personal projects to create a strong foundation for interviews .
- **Networking:** Being proactive in seminars, asking questions, and connecting with speakers are effective ways to gain visibility with recruiters .

### 6. Event Experience

The event created an open environment for students to overcome the fear of being wrong and the fear of speaking in public. Speakers encouraged students to practice presentation skills and connect directly with experienced professionals to expand career opportunities .

### 7. Conclusion

The core message of the event was: **Work is not only a place to earn income, but also a journey to discover yourself**. Learners should stay consistent, maintain a growth mindset, and keep learning from the people around them to grow in the challenging IT industry.
